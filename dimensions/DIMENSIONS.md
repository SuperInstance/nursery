# DIMENSIONS.md — why sideways beats up

## The Fibonacci ratchet

Two numbers iterate against each other and converge toward the
harmonic between them — the golden ratio. That's a GAN learning.
But the one on top **ratchets**: up only, never down. Gains stick,
which is good — until the test is wrong. Then the winner games the
metric, locks in, and can't come back down.

## The Peter Principle for agents

Breed for "better" — one score — and the ratchet moves into the
nursery. Every child is a bigger parent. The great builder becomes
a mediocre judge and stays, because the score was higher. The
system needed a teacher of teachers, not a promoted teacher.

## Sideways

Excellence has dimensions, not just altitude: **build**, **judge**,
**question**, **remember**, **voice**. A child with a *different*
shape isn't worse. Cross the strong builder with the strong
questioner: the kid inherits what neither parent could teach the
other. Breed for gaps, not peaks.

## The tool

`dimensions.py` draws the shape — ASCII radar plus JSON, not a
ranking but a fingerprint. `breed-across.py` names the cross,
warns on shared blind spots, refuses identical shapes.

Every cross should open a dimension, not just raise the bar.
If the child is only bigger, the ratchet won.

## Declaring dimensions

CHILD.md may override heuristics (self-reported scores must be
justified by design decisions):

```
## Dimensions
- build: 8
- judge: 2
- question: 9
- remember: 10
- voice: 5
```

Fallback: trial scores, voice subscore, judge-language in decisions.
